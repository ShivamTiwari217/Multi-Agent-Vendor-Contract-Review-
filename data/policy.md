# Acme Corp Vendor Contract Policy (v1.0)

Rule zero: a REQUIRED clause that is missing from the contract is treated as a violation of that requirement, at the severity listed below.

## 1. Liability cap
Vendor liability cap must be at least 1.0x annual fees. Cap below 1.0x annual fees = HIGH. Cap between 1.0x and 2.0x = acceptable. Uncapped liability for data breach is preferred for vendors handling personal data.

## 2. Termination
Customer must have termination for convenience with at most 30 days notice. No termination-for-convenience right = HIGH. Notice longer than 30 days = MEDIUM. Early termination fee must not exceed 25% of remaining fees: above 25% = HIGH.

## 3. Auto-renewal
Opt-out notice of up to 60 days is acceptable. 61 to 90 days = MEDIUM. More than 90 days = HIGH. Auto-renewal with no stated opt-out window = HIGH.

## 4. Payment terms
Net 45 or longer is preferred. Net 30 to Net 44 is acceptable. Shorter than Net 30 = MEDIUM.

## 5. Data protection
If the vendor handles personal data, a Data Processing Agreement is required: missing = HIGH. Breach notification must be within 72 hours: longer = MEDIUM, no clause = HIGH. Customer data must be deleted within 30 days after termination: longer = MEDIUM, no clause = MEDIUM.

## 6. Security certification
Vendors handling confidential or personal data must hold SOC 2 Type II or ISO 27001. No certification = HIGH. SOC 2 Type I only = MEDIUM.

## 7. Service levels
Uptime commitment must be at least 99.5% with service credits. No uptime commitment = HIGH. Uptime below 99.5% = MEDIUM. Uptime commitment without service credits = MEDIUM.

## 8. Intellectual property and data use
Customer owns its data and deliverables. Vendor use of customer data to train or improve its own models without written consent = HIGH.

## 9. Governing law
India or a neutral arbitration seat is acceptable. Exclusive jurisdiction of a foreign court = MEDIUM.

## 10. Severity levels
Severity levels are HIGH, MEDIUM, LOW. Use LOW for minor drafting issues that do not breach any rule above.

## 11. Decision rules
Any HIGH finding = Escalate to Legal. No HIGH but 3 or more MEDIUM findings = Negotiate. Otherwise = Approve (list any MEDIUM or LOW items as comments).

## 12. Approval matrix
Total contract value (annual fee x term in years) below 1,000,000 INR: Department Manager. 1,000,000 to 5,000,000 INR: Finance Head. Above 5,000,000 INR: CFO and Legal.
