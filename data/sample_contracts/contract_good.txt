SERVICES AGREEMENT between Acme Corp and Northwind Analytics Pvt Ltd

1. Services. Northwind provides a hosted analytics platform (the "Service") to Acme.
2. Fees. Annual subscription fee is INR 1,200,000. Term is 24 months from the Effective Date.
3. Payment. Invoices are payable within 45 days of receipt.
4. Renewal. The agreement renews for successive 12-month terms unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 45 days before the term ends.
5. Termination. Acme may terminate for convenience on 30 days written notice. An early termination fee of 15% of remaining fees applies.
6. Liability. Each party's aggregate liability is capped at 2x the annual fees. Liability for breach of data protection obligations is uncapped.
7. Service levels. Northwind commits to 99.9% monthly uptime. Service credits of 5% of monthly fees apply per 0.1% shortfall.
8. Data protection. Northwind processes personal data under the attached Data Processing Agreement. Northwind will notify Acme of any personal data breach within 48 hours. On termination, Northwind deletes all Acme data within 30 days.
9. Security. Northwind maintains a current SOC 2 Type II report and ISO 27001 certification.
10. Data ownership. Acme owns all Acme data and outputs. Northwind will not use Acme data for any purpose other than providing the Service.
11. Governing law. Laws of India. Disputes by arbitration seated in Mumbai.
