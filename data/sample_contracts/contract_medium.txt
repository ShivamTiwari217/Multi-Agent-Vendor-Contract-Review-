SOFTWARE SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT between Acme Corp and Brightline HR Software Ltd

1. Services. Brightline provides a cloud HR management platform.
2. Fees. Annual fee is INR 800,000. Initial term is 24 months.
3. Payment. Invoices are due within 20 days of the invoice date.
4. Renewal. The agreement auto-renews for 12-month terms unless Acme gives written notice of non-renewal at least 90 days before expiry.
5. Termination. Acme may terminate for convenience on 30 days written notice, subject to an early termination fee of 20% of remaining fees.
6. Liability. Brightline's total liability is limited to fees paid in the 12 months before the claim (1x annual fees).
7. Service levels. Brightline commits to 99.5% monthly uptime, with service credits for any shortfall.
8. Data protection. Brightline processes employee personal data under the attached Data Processing Agreement. Breach notification within 72 hours. Data is deleted within 30 days after termination.
9. Security. Brightline holds a SOC 2 Type II report.
10. Data ownership. Acme owns all of its data. Brightline will not use Acme data to train models.
11. Governing law. This agreement is governed by English law, and the courts of London have exclusive jurisdiction.
