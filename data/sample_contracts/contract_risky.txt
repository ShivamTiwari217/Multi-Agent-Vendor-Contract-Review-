MASTER AGREEMENT between Acme Corp and QuickScore AI Labs Inc.

1. Services. QuickScore provides an AI-based applicant scoring service. QuickScore will process applicant personal data supplied by Acme.
2. Fees. Annual fee is INR 2,500,000. Term is 36 months.
3. Payment. Invoices are payable within 30 days.
4. Renewal. This agreement automatically renews for 12-month terms unless Acme gives notice of non-renewal at least 120 days before the end of the then-current term.
5. Termination. Neither party may terminate for convenience. If Acme terminates early for any reason it must pay 60% of all remaining fees.
6. Liability. QuickScore's aggregate liability shall not exceed the fees paid by Acme in the three months preceding the claim.
7. Service levels. QuickScore will use commercially reasonable efforts to keep the Service available.
8. Security. QuickScore applies industry standard security measures.
9. Data use. QuickScore may use Acme data and applicant data to improve and train its models and products.
10. Governing law. Laws of Delaware, USA, with exclusive jurisdiction of the Delaware courts.
